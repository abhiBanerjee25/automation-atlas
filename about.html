About this section

Who built it

This section was built by Abhik Banerjee, a Computer Science Engineering student at Heritage Institute of Technology, Kolkata. It was built as part of the Vyohm AI Software Track virtual assignment, which asks candidates to build an AI content engine for their own section of this site.

Who checks the answers

Every published answer is reviewed by a real, independent expert: Mr.Mayukh Chatterjee, a business owner who uses automation tools in their own business. Mayukh has agreed in writing to be named.

The reviewer is not employed or paid by any AI provider this section covers. If that ever changes, the page will say so.

How AI is used

  • AI finds questions that Indian business owners ask about AI automation tools.
  • AI drafts an answer to each question and links to the sources it used, with the date each source was checked.
  • A human expert checks the draft. The expert can approve, edit or reject it.
  • A person checks the title, description, image text and facts before anything is published.
  • Only approved answers are published. Each page says it was drafted with AI.

AI does the drafting. People decide what gets published and what is shared in public.

Who owns this site

AI Atlas India is owned by Vyohm AI Private Limited, which also sells AI products. We cover every provider, Vyohm included, by the same rules.

If a Vyohm product fits a question, it appears next to the alternatives and is labelled as owned by Vyohm. It is judged by the same criteria as every other provider. There are no paid placements, affiliate links or sponsored rankings in this section.
